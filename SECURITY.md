# VANTABLACK: Security Policy & Vulnerability Disclosure

## Ethical Reporting
VANTABLACK is an industrial security audit tool. We prioritize the security of the orchestrator itself.

## Reporting a Vulnerability
If you discover a vulnerability within the VANTABLACK orchestrator or its sub-systems:
1. Do **not** open a public issue.
2. Send a detailed report to `security@vantablack.internal` or via your clandestine contact channel.

We will work with you to resolve the issue as quickly as possible and will ensure proper credit in the clandestine release notes.
